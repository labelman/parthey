Our Signature Offering

The Two-Continent Journey

This is the experience that only we can offer, because both places are genuinely ours.

The same values in two completely different expressions: the ancient agricultural landscape of the Jura, where a family has farmed the same floodplain for generations, and the living ecological frontier of coastal Brazil, where a new food culture is being built in the shadow of the Atlantic Forest. The same conversation about land, care, and what endures — carried across an ocean.

How It Works

The Journey

An Outline

Three to Four Weeks

Week 1

Arrive in France — Chateau de Parthey

Settle into the chateau. The Jura at its own pace — the farm, the fruitière, the wine country, the cave where the cheese ages. Seven nights in Franche-Comté with full Comté immersion programming.

Transit

Optional Paris or Geneva Interlude

A night or two between continents, if you want it. We can suggest specific things worth doing in both cities that connect to the themes of the journey.

Week 2–3

Arrive in Brazil — Florianópolis

Into the other world. The island, the Atlantic Forest, the farmers at the feiras, the agroecological cooperative farms of Santa Catarina. Seven to ten nights with full ecological and cultural immersion programming.

Optional

Documentary Extension

For those who want to make something as well as experience it — a documentary component can be added to either or both legs. You leave with your own film. We guide the process.

Return

What You Carry Home

Two landscapes, two food cultures, two expressions of the same underlying question — what does it mean to tend something well over time. The rest is yours.
