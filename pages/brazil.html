Florianópolis, Santa Catarina

Brazil — At the Edge of the Atlantic Forest

Florianópolis is an island city where the Atlantic Forest meets the South Atlantic — one of Brazil's most biodiverse coastal environments and home to a growing network of small agroecological farmers who are quietly building one of the world's most interesting food cultures.

We live here. Our experiences are built around our own relationships — with the farmers at the feiras, with the Rede Ecovida cooperative network, with the researchers at UFSC, with the island's extraordinary landscape. You will not be taken on a tour. You will be brought into a world.
