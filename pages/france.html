Franche-Comté, France

Chateau de Parthey

Chateau de Parthey stands in Choisey, near Dole, in the heart of the Franche-Comté region — a few kilometres from the borders of the Comté appellation, one of the world's last intact artisanal food systems. The property has been in the same family for generations. Our tenant farmer Claude has worked the surrounding land for decades, including the floodplain field that was not sold when industrial concerns bought up the fertile plain around it.

The chateau accommodates 6–9 guests and is available May through October. What we offer goes well beyond the house itself.

Day by Day

The Comté Week in Detail

Arrive

Arrival at the Chateau

Settle in. Welcome dinner prepared with the farm's vegetables and cheese from the fruitière. First glass of Jura Chardonnay. The week begins slowly, as it should.

Day 2

A Morning on the Farm

Private access to the working farm. Walk the herd, see the floodplain field that was not sold when the industrial operations bought up the plain around it. Understand what it means to hold on.

Day 3

The Fruitière

Early morning at the cooperative. Watch the milk arrive and the transformation begin. Meet the people who have kept this ancient pooling system alive against every economic incentive to abandon it.

Day 4

Jura Wine Country

The Côtes du Jura at a small domaine that works as the Jura always has. Chardonnay, Savagnin, Poulsard, Trousseau. Lunch with wine, the slow kind.

Day 5

The Affineur's Cave

Into the cave where the wheels age — some for four months, some for two years. Tasting across the spectrum. The cheese you had on arrival now makes complete sense.

Day 6

Free Day

Dole, Arbois, Salins-les-Bains, the Lac de Chalain — or nothing at all. The garden, a book, the last of the afternoon wine.

Depart

Farewell Breakfast

One last morning. What you take with you is yours to keep.

"A place where time moves at the speed of the land."
