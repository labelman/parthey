Day by Day

The Comté Week in Detail

Arrive

Arrival at the Chateau

Settle in. Welcome dinner prepared with Claude's vegetables and cheese from the fruitière. First glass of Jura Chardonnay. The week begins slowly, as it should.

Day 2

A Morning with Claude

Private access to the working farm. Walk the herd, see the floodplain field that Claude refused to sell when the industrial operations bought up the plain around it. Understand what it means to hold on.

Day 3

The Fruitière

Early morning at the cooperative where Claude's milk arrives with that of the other small farms. Watch the transformation begin. Meet the people who have kept this ancient pooling system alive against every economic incentive to abandon it.

Day 4

Jura Wine Country

The Côtes du Jura — Chardonnay, Savagnin, Poulsard, Trousseau — at a small domaine that works the way the Jura always has. Lunch with wine, the slow kind.

Day 5

The Affineur's Cave

Into the cave where the wheels age — some for four months, some for two years. Tasting across the spectrum. The cheese you had on arrival now makes complete sense.

Day 6

Free Day

Dole, Arbois, Salins-les-Bains, the Lac de Chalain — or nothing at all. The garden, a book, the last of the afternoon wine.

Depart

Farewell Breakfast

One last morning. What you take with you is yours to keep.
